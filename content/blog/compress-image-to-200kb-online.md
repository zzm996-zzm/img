## Quick answer

To compress an image to 200KB online, open the [OnlineBox Image Compressor](/image-compressor), upload your image, enter 200 as the target KB value and download the compressed JPG. The compression runs locally in your browser, so the original image does not need to upload to a server.

## Step by step

1. Open the [image compressor](/image-compressor).
2. Drop a JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC or HEIF image into the upload area.
3. Enter 200 KB as the target file size.
4. Click Compress image.
5. Check the downloaded file size. If it is still too large for your upload form, try a lower target such as 180 KB.

## When 200KB compression is useful

Many application forms, student portals, job sites and profile pages ask for photos under a strict size limit. A 200KB image is usually small enough for upload forms while still keeping enough quality for headshots, ID-style photos and document images.

For product photos or social media images, you may prefer 500KB or 1MB if the platform allows it. Smaller files load faster, but very aggressive compression can make text, faces and product details look soft.

## Tips for better quality

Start with the largest acceptable target. If the site allows 500KB, do not force the image down to 100KB. Crop unnecessary background before compressing. If the image has exact dimension requirements, use the [image resizer](/image-resizer) before or after compression.

## FAQ

### Does the image upload anywhere?

No. The OnlineBox compressor uses browser APIs for the core compression workflow. The server delivers the page, but your image stays on your device.

### Can I compress PNG to 200KB?

Yes. The compressor can load PNG and export a smaller JPG. This is useful when file size matters more than preserving transparency.

### Can I compress iPhone HEIC photos?

Yes. Use the browser-based HEIC support on the [image compressor](/image-compressor) or convert the file first with the [HEIC to JPG converter](/image-converter).
