## Quick answer

## Why HEIC causes upload problems

iPhones often save photos as HEIC or HEIF. These formats are efficient, but some websites, forms, CMS tools and older desktop apps only accept JPG or PNG. Converting HEIC to JPG makes the photo easier to upload and share.

## How to convert HEIC to JPG

1. Open the [HEIC to JPG converter](/image-converter).
2. Choose JPG as the output format.
3. Upload your `.heic` or `.heif` file.
4. Click Convert image.
5. Download the converted JPG.

## What format should you choose?

Choose JPG for forms, profile photos, marketplace listings and general sharing. Choose PNG if you need a lossless screenshot-style export. Choose WebP for web assets when the destination supports it.

If the converted JPG is still too large, use the [image compressor](/image-compressor) to reduce it to 200KB, 500KB or another target size.

## FAQ

### Does the HEIC photo upload to OnlineBox?

No. The conversion runs locally in your browser for the core workflow.

### Can I convert HEIC to PNG?

Yes. The image converter supports JPG, PNG and WebP output options.

### Why is my converted image larger?

JPG and PNG use different compression methods. If file size matters, compress the converted image after conversion.
